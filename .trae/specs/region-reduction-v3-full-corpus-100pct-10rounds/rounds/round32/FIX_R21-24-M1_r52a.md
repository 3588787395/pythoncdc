## 4. Criterion + its population across the corpus

### 4.1 Which criterion I used, and why (region list vs block_to_region)

Module-level helper `_r52_m1_undispatched_struct_entries(gen, blocks)` (generator :371):
returns those `b` that are the **`entry`** of some region `r` in **`gen.regions`** with
`type(r) ∈ RegionASTGenerator._STRUCTURAL_REGION_TYPES` (If/Loop/TryExcept/With/Match — statement-level;
expression BoolOp/Ternary excluded, they have no statement body to emit) whose `id(r)` is in **neither**
`gen._generated_regions` nor `gen._generating_regions` (undispatched ledger). Facts read: region-set
membership + entry identity + dispatch ledger — no offsets, names, or jump targets.

**Why the region LIST and NOT `block_to_region`:** I measured the declared-ownership census first.
`block_to_region` is a **single-value first-write-wins** map, and it does NOT recover entry identity for
6 of the 7 victims:
```
victim 7972: block_to_region owner=TryExceptRegion  .entry_is_self=False  dispatched=False
victim 8294: owner=TryExceptRegion .entry_is_self=False      (same for 8350/8354/8400/8450)
victim 8186: owner=TryExceptRegion .entry_is_self=TRUE   dispatched=False
```
Only @8186 (the TryExceptRegion) keeps entry identity via `block_to_region[b].entry is b`; the six
IfRegion entries collapse onto the enclosing TryExceptRegion, so a `block_to_region`-only guard would
**miss six of the seven** and fail to close the −109. Hence I read `gen.regions` directly. (This is the
opposite of the general preference, and is justified only by this measurement.)
