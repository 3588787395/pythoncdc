## §1 — the criterion (structural) and how it differs from the two landed family members

The target criterion is **on the analyzer side, not the generator side**:

> In `_detect_while_boolop_forward_chain`, an OR operand block whose `conditional_successors`
> fall-through is a `FORWARD_CONDITIONAL_JUMP` block that itself targets the SAME body as the
> current operand — but which the analyzer has already claimed as an independent
> `LoopRegion` — must still be absorbed into the while-condition BoolOp chain when that
> pre-claimed LoopRegion's header/body is exactly the disjunction's shared target. Such a
> "nested loop" cannot be a real `while B: body` nested inside `while A: …`, because in the
> nested reading `B` is evaluated *before* entering the body, whereas in the disjunctive
> reading `B` is short-circuited whenever `A` is true (A jumps straight to the body without
> evaluating B). The two are distinguishable purely by the predecessor set of the body block.

No function names, no literal offsets, no counters/thresholds: the predicate is "operand
jump-target == fall-through operand's jump-target == a single shared body block" plus the
short-circuit-vs-nesting edge-disjointness test on that body's predecessors.

**Distinct from the two landed family criteria:**
- Gate 36 (`efd455859b69d8a1` / `_loop_handle_no_exit_successors`): about a *merge* block also
  entered from the then side. It renders the then side after the if. Does not fire here —
  broker products were byte-identical under it (ADJUDICATION_GATE37 §"Negative").
- Gate 37 (`20fa73b4e8231f0f` / R21-36 continue-arm hoisting): about an *arm ending in a PURE
  back-edge block*. The discriminator there is forward-reachability from `else_blocks` not
  intersecting then-blocks past the tail. `_sync_worker`'s operands are not pure back-edge
  arms and it does not flow through that site (trace: broker product byte-identical under
  gate 37, per ADJUDICATION_GATE37).
- Mine is a different axis entirely: **the while-condition expression reduction in the
  analyzer**, upstream of both. Neither landed criterion touches it (both left the broker
  product unchanged).

**Population: NOT counted — stated honestly.** A safe census requires a corpus-wide scan for
while-conditions whose OR-operand blocks were pre-claimed as nested `LoopRegion`s. That scan
is exactly the change's regression surface (every `while X or Y:` / `while X and Y:` unit in
the corpus flows through `_detect_while_boolop_forward_chain`, the single most-guarded
detector in `region_analyzer.py` — it already carries `[B6-while]`, `[R7-B45]`, `[R3-L]`,
`[Phase 3 adv11]`, `[Round 08]` guards). I did not run the scan, so I do not claim a count.
