## Why the round gate (≥1 real pyc) was NOT met
The remaining EXC-1 failures and the broader partial set share a deep root cause: the region reducer **drops/merges structurally-identical consecutive regions**. Concrete evidence:
- `IQData/utils/datetime_func.pyc::change_2str_of_time_2_datetime` and its twin `IQCommon/util/datetime_func.pyc::change_2str_of_time_2_datetime`: two identical-shaped statements
  `source_start = datetime.datetime.strptime(...)`, `source_end = datetime.datetime.strptime(...)` — the decompiler collapsed the second into the first, dropping `source_end` and corrupting the `return`. ~25 instructions missing.
- `fly/common/convert.pyc::getchnstr`: decompiled **source is semantically correct** but bytecode has one **spurious `JUMP_BACKWARD`** before the final `return` (back-edge emitted as explicit jump — violates the "back_edge_block JUMP_BACKWARD is implicit continue, not generated" rule). Affects all loop-ending functions.
- EXC-3 (bare `except` reraise tail) and EXC-4 (loop+try) are likewise region-reducer structural issues.

These require principled fixes in region identification/reduction (not patchwork) and are the recommended focus for subsequent rounds (from hard to easy: with-statement region ownership → duplicate-structure statement collapse → spurious back-jump emission → EXC-3/EXC-4).
