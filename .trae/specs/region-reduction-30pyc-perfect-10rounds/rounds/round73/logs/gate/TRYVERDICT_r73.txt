3. what the numbers say (reported as measured, not as advocacy)
   - 44 of 87 failing units live in code objects with NO exception table at all,
     and 73 of 87 first diverge OUTSIDE any exception region: a nested try-except
     mechanism cannot be the single root cause for those units.
   - 30 / 87 units do have an exception-table difference, and 14 / 87 first diverge
     inside an exception region; those are the genuine try-except sub-family and are
     the ones this round did NOT take on (absm was rejected on ADR-1).
   - product nesting depth never exceeded the original (prod_deeper=0), so the defect
     is not "the product nests too deep" either.
