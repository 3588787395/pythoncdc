ANSWER: (a) and (b) are BOTH live and jointly fatal, and they are downstream of (c):
  (a) CONFIRMED  BAIL7_SLOT literal lines above -- the kwarg-slot loop `return None`.
  (b) CONFIRMED  PRED_IS_CHAIN_TAIL=True / PRED_BACKWARD_EXTENDS=True / tail_chain=[]

      (350, 414 and the region entering at them) never reach the builder.

      (PRED_SHOULD_RECONSTRUCT=True -> PRED_RECON_NONE=True) fails because the
      reconstruct path cannot express KW_NAMES, and the tail's CALL that the builder
      binds to is the OUTER call (total_args=1) rather than the `.format` call owning

and total_args=1, no slot assembly can succeed.  Fixing (b) alone also cannot: measured
by arm sk1 (inert).  The implementer must (i) hand the builder the chain HEAD, (ii) let
it enumerate every value of the statement in bytecode order (member preload siblings +
member ternary values) instead of the "first consecutive slots" precondition, and
(iii) bind the KW_NAMES-owning CALL, not the last CALL in the merge block.
